# Slice 9-B1RA stakeholder decision matrix

## Control statement

This matrix operationalizes the Slice 9-B1R design boundary at `9b92ca001bbbf01ac5dfe5007c131f8dc5157a6e`. It is a RACI/owner map and decision worksheet, not a completed approval. Provider delivery remains disabled, no persistence or schema design is authorized, and no runtime change or deployment occurs.

Status options for every decision owner: `approved`, `approved with conditions`, `rejected`, `requires more information`, `not applicable`. “Not applicable” requires a written rationale accepted by the business sponsor and facilitator.

## Approval workflow

1. Facilitator issues evidence and assigns stable decision IDs.
2. Each accountable owner completes its row and decision-log entries.
3. Conditions and blocking questions receive one owner and one due date each.
4. Cross-owner conflicts are logged; the stronger red line remains until resolved.
5. Data owner checks taxonomy/source consistency; legal/privacy/security verify no boundary broadening.
6. Business sponsor reviews specialist outputs and records design-only go/no-go.
7. Facilitator completes the authorization checklist without converting conditional approval into unconditional approval.
8. Any rejected/missing review, ownerless blocker, overdue condition, red-line breach, or expired review blocks Slice 9-B2.

No status in this matrix authorizes persistence, schema creation in Slice 9-B1RA, runtime changes, provider enforcement, customer communications, or deployment.
