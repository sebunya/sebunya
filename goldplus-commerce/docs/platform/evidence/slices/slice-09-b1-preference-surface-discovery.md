# Slice 9-B1 preference surface discovery

## Scope and method

This is a read-only source audit at baseline `6afc99838545ed6e38d6cf3db9e23bf6e8a0d223`. It does not change runtime behavior, save a preference, activate a provider, send a message, create a migration, or deploy. Searches covered public and account pages, checkout and support forms, policy copy, loyalty and future-programme readiness, admin readiness, HTTP routes and use cases, notification routing, Measurement consent, schemas, migrations, and tests.

Authority levels used below:

- **Guidance only**: copy or a read-only preview; it cannot establish customer state.
- **Service intent only**: contact supplied for a specific order or support purpose; it is not campaign consent.
- **Legacy persisted input**: a real stored setting, but its purpose/channel semantics are too broad to be canonical consent.
- **Measurement consent authority**: authoritative only for the existing analytics, advertising, and personalization destination gates.
- **Provider/platform state**: an external unsubscribe or STOP signal that must override marketing when integrated.
- **Future canonical authority**: the proposed purpose-by-channel consent record; it does not exist yet.

## Required discovery answers

1. **Is there already a real saved preference system?** Yes, `/account/preferences` writes `customer_preferences`, writes a preference audit row, publishes a Measurement preference event, and can write Measurement consent. Its broad flags are legacy persisted inputs, not a complete canonical communication-consent model.
2. **Is there already a provider-enforced consent system?** Measurement has purpose-based destination gating. No dedicated active-source customer messaging preference/suppression contract was found for email, SMS, or WhatsApp provider sends.
3. **Is there already a newsletter system?** There is newsletter-style footer copy, but it explicitly says email updates are opening soon and has no form, persistence, or send.
4. **Does checkout collect marketing consent?** No. Checkout contact data is necessary service/order intent only.
5. **Does support collect campaign consent?** No. Support contact is purpose-limited follow-up intent only.
6. **Does the public Preference Centre save anything?** No. It is static, non-customer-specific guidance and contains no form, API call, browser storage, or provider path.
7. **Does the consent alias create another state?** No. `/consent` redirects to `/preferences` with 303.
8. **Are loyalty, Memory Lane, personalization, or utilization offers active?** No. Their surfaces explicitly describe readiness or concepts only.
9. **Are channel and purpose represented independently in the legacy account form?** No. Channel booleans mix service and optional purposes; the email label explicitly combines receipts, shipping, and occasional offers.
10. **Is withdrawal unified?** No. Legacy unchecking, Measurement withdrawal, support correction, and external WhatsApp unfollow/STOP concepts are not reconciled into one precedence model.
11. **Is audit unified?** No. Preference audits, Measurement consent records, order/support records, and delivery/outbox audit are separate. The account audit use case explicitly does not combine consent audit.
12. **Can product-interest behavior grant communications consent?** No. Product Finder interest writes must remain recommendation-interest signals only.
13. **Is contact validity equivalent to consent?** No. Existing notification eligibility checks establish reachability for an order/service context, not optional campaign permission.
14. **Is there a dedicated External Delivery preference or suppression module?** No dedicated active-source preference/suppression contract was found. Existing notification/outbox paths remain downstream dependencies that must fail closed before customer-provider activation.
15. **Is the legacy success copy demonstrably false?** No. It is rendered only after an authenticated API PUT returns success, and that path writes both preference state and an audit row. The stronger problem is semantic breadth, not a fake save claim.
16. **What is the present source of truth?** There is no single cross-purpose source of truth. Authority is partitioned: orders/support for service intent, legacy account JSON for broad settings, Measurement consent for Measurement destinations, and external provider STOP/unsubscribe as a future overriding source.

## Current source-of-truth assessment

GoldPlus currently has multiple valid but bounded authorities, not one consent authority. Order and support records prove service context. The legacy account preference record proves that a logged-in user submitted broad settings. Measurement records prove the state of Measurement purposes for a fingerprint/user association. None alone can authorize an optional customer message. A future canonical service must reconcile purpose, channel, verified identity, source, withdrawal, suppression, policy, and audit at decision time.
