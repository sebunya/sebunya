# Slice 8-A loyalty and gamification foundation P0

Date: 2026-07-14 (Africa/Kampala)

## Delivered

- Public `/loyalty` foundation page with visible programme-not-active truth.
- Pure immutable loyalty foundation structures with no customer state, persistence or API access.
- Programme setup progress preview, ten quest concepts, non-financial badge states, four tier previews and a consent-gated Memory Lane concept.
- Eight-row utilisation-aware offer matrix and margin-, budget-, support- and approval-led discount governance.
- Deterministic mystery reveal concepts that issue no prize, code, discount or data capture.
- Full launch-readiness and risk-control checklists.
- Protected `/admin/loyalty` operator preview with a mechanic readiness leaderboard and utilisation signal scorecard.
- Read-only preview discovery from the disabled Admin Trust Centre loyalty module.
- Public footer and sitemap discoverability.

## Inactive by design

There is no points ledger, balance, wallet, enrolment, customer tier, badge award, quest completion, Memory Lane history, offer eligibility, discount, coupon, prize, redemption, cashback, identity tracking, inventory/order/customer read, checkout change, provider activation or customer communication send.

## Safeguards

- Quests require identity, consent, ledger, fraud, audit and approved benefit rules before activation.
- Earned and undiscovered badge states require consented history and auditable award rules.
- Memory Lane requires consent, identity, event history, retention policy, privacy review and approved messaging.
- Utilisation-aware offers require approved data contracts, finance/commercial approval, margin floors, budget caps, fair eligibility and support capacity.
- Leaderboards rank mechanics and signals only; they contain no customer names, contact details, balances, spend or eligibility.
- Public copy avoids live-reward, fake urgency, gambling, VIP, cashback and personalised-price claims.

This release creates no financial liability and cannot mutate commerce state.
