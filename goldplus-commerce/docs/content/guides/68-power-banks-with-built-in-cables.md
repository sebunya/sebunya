A power bank with built-in cables is worth it for most people, because the most common reason a power bank fails to help is that the cable was left at home. The trade-offs are that the cables are short, the connectors are fixed, and you need to treat the joint gently. If your phones use the connectors on the unit, the convenience usually wins.

# The advantages

**You cannot forget the cable.** The leads are part of the power bank, so wherever it goes, they go.

**No borrowing.** On campus, at work or at a family gathering during a power cut, you do not need to hunt for the right lead.

**Several phone types from one unit.** Some models carry more than one connector, which helps in a household or office where people have different phones.

**Less clutter.** One item in the bag instead of a power bank plus a tangle of cables.

# The trade-offs

**Short leads.** Built-in cables are kept short so they tuck away. You will usually hold the phone close to the power bank, or rest them together.

**Fixed connectors.** If you change to a phone with a different port, the built-in cable may no longer fit it. Check the connectors against every phone you own, and against the phone you are likely to buy next.

**Wear at the joint.** The point where a cable meets the body takes the most bending. If a built-in cable is damaged, you cannot simply swap it. Many such power banks still have ports you can use with a separate cable, but check the exact model.

**Speed depends on the cable too.** A built-in cable has to support the same fast charging standard as the power bank and phone. The listing for the model tells you what it supports.

# Match the connectors to your phones

- **USB-C:** most newer Android phones and the latest iPhones.
- **Lightning:** older iPhones.
- **Micro-USB, often called V8:** many older and budget phones.
- **USB-A:** the larger rectangular plug, used on many cables and older devices.

# How to make built-in cables last

1. **Pull by the plug, never by the cable.** Hold the connector head when you unplug.
2. **Do not bend the cable sharply** at the joint, and do not wrap it tightly.
3. **Put the cables back in their slots** before the power bank goes in a bag or pocket.
4. **Do not let the phone hang from the cable.** Rest the phone on a surface or hold both together.
5. **Keep connectors clean and dry.** Dust and moisture in the plug can stop charging.

# Is it right for you?

Built-in cables suit you if you often forget leads, share with people who have different phones, or want one tidy item in your bag. A power bank without them suits you if you want a longer cable or switch phones often.
