A GoldPlus gift card is the easy present for someone who needs a charger, earbuds or a power bank but you are not sure which. You choose a card and an amount, pay with Mobile Money or card, and we text it to them. They open it from our text and scratch to reveal the code. The card does not expire.

Everything happens on the [gift card page](/gift-card/buy).

# Step 1: choose an occasion

Pick a design for the occasion. There are cards for birthdays, thank you, congratulations, with love and anniversaries, plus seasonal ones such as Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Easter, Eid, Christmas, New Year and season's greetings. There is also a general "a gift for you" card.

# Step 2: choose how much

Pick one of the set amounts, 20,000, 50,000, 100,000 or 200,000 Uganda shillings, or choose **Other** and type your own amount in UGX.

# Step 3: say who it is for

- **Their name.**
- **Their mobile number.** This is optional.
- **Send the code to me instead.** Tick this if you would rather give it to them yourself, for example in a birthday card or in person. The code comes to you as soon as you pay, to give whenever you like.

# Step 4: make it personal (optional)

**A note.** Add a short note on the card, up to 160 characters. For their safety, notes cannot include links or phone numbers.

**A photo.** You can put a photo on the card instead of the picture. Only they and you see it. Use a photo you are happy for them to keep. They can remove it from their card, and so can you or we, at any time.

# Step 5: choose when it arrives

- **Now.**
- **On a day.** Pick a date, and the card is texted that morning, from 9am. Good for birthdays and anniversaries you want to plan ahead.

# Step 6: pay

Add your name, which appears on the card as "From", and your mobile number for your receipt. Then pay with Mobile Money or card on Pesapal's secure page.

The card is made once your payment is confirmed.

# What the person receives

They get a text from us. They open the card from the text and scratch to reveal the code.

No smartphone or no data? The text has the code in it too, so it works on a phone without internet.

# Good to know

- **It does not expire.** There is no rush to use it.
- **Check a balance.** If you already have a card, you can check its balance from the gift card page.
- **Questions about spending it?** Ask us on WhatsApp on 0705 004545 or from our [support page](/support), and we will walk you through it.

# Ideas for when a gift card works best

- A student going back to campus who will need cables, a charger or a flash drive.
- A relative upcountry who can choose for themselves.
- A colleague's birthday when you do not know their phone model.
- A thank-you that is more useful than flowers.
