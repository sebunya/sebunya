Almost every phone sold in Uganda charges through one of three plugs: **USB-C**, **Lightning** or **Micro-USB**. Look at the port at the bottom of your phone, match its shape, and you know which cable you need. Then check the other end, the one that goes into the charger.

# The three phone plugs

## USB-C (Type-C)

A small oval plug with rounded ends. It goes in either way up, so there is no wrong side. Most Android phones from the last several years use it, including recent Samsung, Tecno, Infinix, itel and Xiaomi models. iPhones from the iPhone 15 onwards use USB-C too, and so do many tablets, laptops, earbud cases and power banks.

## Lightning

A thin, flat plug with exposed metal contacts on both faces. It also goes in either way up. Lightning is Apple's own plug, used on iPhones from the iPhone 5 up to the iPhone 14 series, and on some iPads and older AirPods cases.

## Micro-USB (V8)

A small plug shaped like a squashed trapezium: wider on one side than the other. It only fits one way up, so if it does not go in, turn it over rather than pushing. In Uganda it is often called "V8". It is found on older and budget phones, feature phones, Bluetooth speakers, MiFis, radios and many small gadgets.

# Now check the other end

A cable has two ends, and the second one has to fit your charger, power bank or computer.

- **USB-A** is the older, larger rectangular plug. It fits most standard chargers, computers, car chargers and power banks.
- **USB-C** at the charger end fits chargers and power banks that have a USB-C port. These are usually the ones that support USB Power Delivery (PD) fast charging.

So a cable is named by both ends: "USB-A to USB-C", "USB-C to USB-C", "USB-C to Lightning", "USB-A to Micro-USB". Look at the ports on your charger before you choose.

# Which is best for fast charging?

The plug shape alone does not make charging fast. Fast charging needs the charger, the cable and the phone to support the same standard.

- For an iPhone with Lightning, fast charging needs a **USB-C to Lightning** cable and a USB-C PD charger. A USB-A to Lightning cable charges at normal speed.
- For a USB-C phone, a **USB-C to USB-C** cable with a PD charger is the usual route to fast charging. Some phones also fast charge over USB-A with their maker's own system.
- **Micro-USB** phones generally charge more slowly than modern USB-C phones. A good cable still helps them charge as fast as the phone allows.

# Common mix-ups

- **USB-C is not the same as USB 3.0.** USB-C is the shape of the plug. USB 3.0 is a data speed. A USB-C cable may or may not carry fast data.
- **"Type-C" and "USB-C" are the same thing.**
- **"V8" and "Micro-USB" are the same thing** for phone cables.
- **One cable does not fit all.** Adapters exist, but a cable with the right plug at each end is simpler and less likely to work loose.
