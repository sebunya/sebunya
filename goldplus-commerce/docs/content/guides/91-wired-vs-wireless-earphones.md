Choose wired earphones if you want something simple that never needs charging and your phone has a headphone jack. Choose wireless if you want freedom from the cable and do not mind charging one more thing. Many people end up owning both: wireless for the day, a wired pair in the bag as a backup.

# First check: does your phone have a headphone jack?

Wired earphones like our [GP-H06](/products/goldplus-earphones-gp-h06-type-pin) use a 3.5mm plug. Look at the bottom or top edge of your phone for a small round socket.

- **If it has one,** wired earphones plug straight in.
- **If it does not,** you need an adapter that suits your phone's charging port, or wireless earphones instead.

Many newer phones have dropped the jack, so it is worth checking before you buy.

# Why choose wired

- **Nothing to charge.** They take their power from the phone, so they work during a long power cut as long as your phone does.
- **Nothing to pair.** Plug in and listen. Handy if you switch between devices, such as a phone and a laptop that has a jack.
- **Hard to lose one side.** There is no single earbud to drop on a taxi floor.
- **Usually cheaper.** A good wired pair costs less than most wireless pairs.

Our GP-H06's listing describes it as having extra bass, a mic for calls and inline buttons. Nothing to charge, nothing to pair.

# Why choose wireless

- **No cable.** Nothing to tangle in your pocket or catch on a bag strap.
- **More freedom.** Leave the phone on the desk or in a bag and move around.
- **Phone without a jack.** Wireless works with any phone that has Bluetooth.
- **Extras.** Depending on the model: touch controls, a battery display on the case, noise reduction for calls.

The trade-off is charging. Wireless earbuds have small batteries, so you charge the case regularly. Our wireless listings give the figures, for example 4 hours of play time for the [GP-W03](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp-w03) and [GP-W05](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp-w05), with the case recharging them between uses.

# Comparing the two for everyday situations

**Long trips upcountry.** Wireless earbuds with a full case can cover a long journey. A wired pair is a good backup that never runs flat on its own.

**Calls at work.** Both can work well. Wired earphones with a mic are simple and reliable. Wireless lets you move around while you talk.

**Gym or walking.** Wireless is more comfortable when you move a lot, because there is no cable to pull.

**Sharing with a child or a friend.** Wired is simpler: no pairing, and nothing small to lose.

**Sound.** Both can sound good. How a particular pair sounds depends on its design more than on whether it has a cable.

**Using a laptop or desktop.** Wired earphones plug into a computer's headphone jack with no setup. Wireless works too, as long as the computer has Bluetooth.

# Look after either kind

- Wired: wrap the cable loosely, and pull the plug, not the cable, when you unplug.
- Wireless: keep earbuds in their case when not in use, and keep the charging contacts clean.
