A power bank is safe in everyday use when it is kept cool, dry and undamaged. The warning signs are real heat, swelling, cracks, a burning or chemical smell, and hissing. If you notice any of them, stop using and charging the power bank straight away.

# Heat is the main enemy

Lithium-ion cells dislike heat. A little warmth while charging or discharging is normal. Heat that makes the power bank uncomfortable to hold is not.

Avoid the common heat traps:

- **Never charge on a bed, sofa or under a pillow.** Soft surfaces trap heat. Charge on a hard, open surface.
- **Keep it out of direct sun,** including on a car dashboard, a boda seat or a window sill.
- **Do not leave it in a parked car** during the day.
- **Do not cover it** with clothes or blankets while it works.

# Swelling: stop at once

A swollen power bank has a bulging case, a gap at the seams, or no longer sits flat on a table. Swelling means gas is building up inside a cell. It is a fire risk.

If you see swelling:

1. Stop using and charging it immediately.
2. Unplug it and move it away from anything that burns, onto a hard surface such as concrete or tiles, if you can do so safely.
3. Do not press, puncture, open or try to flatten it.
4. Do not put it in household rubbish or a fire. Ask about safe disposal for batteries where you live.

# Damage and water

- **Drops:** after a hard fall, check for cracks and dents. A cracked power bank should not be used.
- **Water:** if one gets wet, do not charge it. Unplug it and let it dry fully before deciding whether to use it. If in doubt, replace it.
- **Bent or loose ports:** a wobbly connector can short or spark. Stop using the port.

# Charge it the right way

- Use a charger and cable in good condition.
- Unplug once it is full, rather than leaving it on charge for days.
- Do not use a damaged or fraying cable, built-in or separate.
- Watch it during the first few charges of a new unit, and charge it where you would notice a problem.

# Storing a power bank

If you will not use a power bank for weeks:

- **Store it about half charged.** A fully empty or fully charged cell ages faster in storage.
- **Top it up every few months,** so it does not drain to empty.
- **Keep it cool and dry,** away from children and pets.
- **Keep metal away from the ports,** such as keys and coins in the same pocket.
