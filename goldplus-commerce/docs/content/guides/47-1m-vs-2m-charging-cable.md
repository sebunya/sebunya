Buy a **1 m cable** if you charge near the socket, carry the cable in a bag, or use it in a car. Buy a **2 m cable** if the socket is across the room from where you sit or sleep. Length makes little difference to charging speed with a well-made cable, so choose for where you will use it.

# Where 1 m works best

- **At a desk or table,** with the charger on the same wall as you.
- **In a bag or pocket.** A shorter cable is lighter and tangles less.
- **With a power bank,** where the phone sits right next to it.
- **In the car,** from the 12V socket to a phone holder on the dashboard.
- **When you want the most charging speed,** because a shorter cable has slightly less resistance.

# Where 2 m works best

- **Charging in bed,** when the only socket is near the floor or on the far wall.
- **On the sofa or at a dining table** without moving the furniture.
- **In shared spaces,** like an office or a family sitting room where one socket serves several people.
- **When you need to use the phone while it charges,** without hunching over the socket.

A longer cable also lets you keep the charger on the floor or a shelf, away from water and children, while the phone stays in reach.

# Does a longer cable charge slower?

A longer wire has a little more resistance, so a 2 m cable loses slightly more power than a 1 m cable of the same build. With a well-made cable rated for your charging, the difference is small for everyday phone charging.

It matters more with cheap cables. A long cable with thin wires can lose enough voltage that the phone charges noticeably slower. If you want a long cable, it is worth getting one with a proper rating and a solid copper core.

# Durability

Longer cables have more length to tangle, step on and catch on furniture. A braided jacket helps here. Short cables carried every day suffer more from being bent at the plug. Either way, the plug joint is the weak point; pull from the plug, not the cable.

> A long cable stretched tight across a walkway is easy to trip on, and the yank can damage the phone's port as well as the cable. Run it along the wall or under furniture.

# How to choose the right length

1. Sit or lie where you usually use your phone while charging.
2. Measure from the socket to your hand, following the path the cable will take, not a straight line.
3. If that is under about 80 cm, a 1 m cable will do. If it is longer, go for 2 m.
4. Check the plugs: your phone's port and your charger's port.
