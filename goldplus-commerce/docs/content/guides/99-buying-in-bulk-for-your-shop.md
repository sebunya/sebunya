If you run a shop, a stall or a business, you can order GoldPlus products by the carton from our [bulk orders page](/bulk). Choose how many cartons you need of each product, send your list, and our team takes it from there. Bulk orders are supplied to order, so they do not depend on what is in our shop that day.

# How bulk ordering works

- **By the carton.** For most products you choose a number of cartons.
- **Flash drives and memory cards by the piece.** For these, the number you enter is pieces, not cartons.
- **Supplied to order.** Your bulk order is not limited by what happens to be on our shelf.
- **Carton sizes are confirmed.** On the bulk page, carton sizes are marked "size to confirm", so check with our team how many pieces are in a carton before you rely on it.

# Three ways to build your list

## 1. Pick products on the page

Search by name or model code, or filter by category: power devices, sound devices, storage devices, car accessories and PC accessories. Use the plus and minus buttons to set the number of cartons for each product. Tick **Only my list** to see just what you have chosen.

## 2. Paste a list of codes

If you already know what you want, this is the fastest way. Paste one product per line: the code, then the number of cartons. For example:

- GP-C08, 5
- GP-C08 x5

You can paste two columns straight from a spreadsheet, which is handy if you keep your stock list in Excel or Google Sheets. Remember that for flash drives and memory cards the number is pieces.

## 3. Only have a budget?

Not sure what to stock? Type your budget into your list and tell us what you sell. Our team builds the list for you.

This is useful if you are opening a new shop, adding a new product line, or want a mix that suits your customers without working out every product yourself.

# How much can you order?

You can order up to 500 cartons of a product from the bulk page. If you need more, send the list anyway and our team confirms supply.

# After you send your list

Already sent a list? You can check its status from the bulk page.

# If the bulk page does not work on your phone

The bulk page needs JavaScript. If it will not load properly on your phone or browser, you can still [request a quote](/quote-request) with our simple form, or message us on WhatsApp on 0705 004545.

# Tips for a smooth bulk order

- **Use model codes.** The code is on every product listing and on the bulk page. It avoids confusion between similar products.
- **Confirm carton sizes** with our team before you plan your prices.
- **Keep a copy of your list.** If you paste it from a spreadsheet, keep that file so you can check it against what arrives.
- **Check what your customers ask for.** A short list of what sells well for you is a better starting point than a long list of guesses.
