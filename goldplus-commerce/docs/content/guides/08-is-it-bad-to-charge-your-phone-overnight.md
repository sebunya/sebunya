No, charging your phone overnight is not bad for a modern phone, as long as it is charging somewhere cool with a decent charger and cable. The phone stops charging when the battery is full. The one small cost is that a battery sitting at 100% for many hours, especially when warm, ages a little faster than one that is cool and part-charged. A setting on most recent phones takes care of that.

# Why it will not "overcharge"

Every phone has a charging circuit that controls how much power goes into the battery. When the battery is full, it stops charging and only tops up now and then as the level drifts down. The charger in the wall does not decide this; the phone does.

So the old fear of a phone "overcharging" all night does not apply to today's phones with a working charger and cable.

# What actually ages the battery overnight

Two things, and they are worse together:

- **Heat.** A phone charging under a pillow, on a mattress or inside a thick case can get warm and stay warm for hours.
- **Hours at 100%.** Lithium-ion batteries age a little faster when they are held full, and more so when they are warm.

Neither will kill your battery in a week. Over a year or two of nightly charging, they add up.

# The setting that fixes most of it

Most recent phones can learn when you usually unplug and hold the battery below full until shortly before then, or cap charging at a lower level.

- **iPhone:** open Settings, then Battery, and look for Optimised Battery Charging or a charging limit option. The exact wording depends on your model and iOS version.
- **Android:** open Settings, then Battery, and look for options such as adaptive charging, battery protection or a charge limit. Names and places differ by brand, and some phones do not have one.

If your phone has one of these, turn it on and forget about it.

# How to charge overnight safely

- **Charge on a hard surface.** A bedside table or a shelf, not the bed, a pillow or a sofa cushion. Heat needs somewhere to go.
- **Take off a thick case** if the phone gets warm while charging.
- **Use a charger with a printed output label**, such as 5V⎓2A, and a cable in good condition. Replace frayed or loose cables.
- **Do not cover the charger** with clothes or bedding. Chargers get warm too.
- **Keep it away from water** and from where children or pets can pull the cable.
- **Never charge a swollen battery.** If the back is lifting or the screen is pushing out, stop charging it altogether.

# What about power cuts and surges?

When the power goes off and comes back, a charger in a wall socket goes through that change. A good charger is designed for this, but a cheap unmarked one may not handle it as well. # Do you need to charge every night?

Not necessarily. If your phone ends the day at 50%, you could charge it in the morning while you get ready instead. Short top-ups during the day are just as good for the battery as one long charge at night. Do what fits your routine.
