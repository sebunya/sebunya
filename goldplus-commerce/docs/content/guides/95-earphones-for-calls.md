If you spend your day on the phone, choose earphones by three things: a microphone that picks up your voice clearly, enough talk time to last your working day, and some form of call noise reduction if you take calls in noisy places. Comfort matters too, because call-heavy days mean hours of wear.

# What to look for on a listing

## A microphone

It sounds obvious, but check it. Wireless earbuds normally have built-in microphones. With wired earphones, look for a mic on the cable. Our [GP-H06](/products/goldplus-earphones-gp-h06-type-pin) wired earphones list a mic for calls and inline buttons.

## Talk time

Talk time is how long wireless earphones last on calls, which can differ from music time. Our listings include:

- [GP03BT](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp03bt): 5 hours talk time.
- [GP-W04](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp-w04): 4 to 5 hours talk time.

Between calls, the charging case tops wireless earbuds back up, so a full day is possible if you put them away during breaks.

## Call noise reduction

Some earphones process the sound from their microphones to reduce background noise, so the person you are calling hears your voice more than the traffic. This is often called ENC. Our listings describe it in their own words: the GP-W03 lists "HD call" and "noise elimination"; the GP-W04 and GP-W05 list "intelligent noise reduction".

These listings do not say exactly how the processing works. If that matters for your work, ask us before you buy.

## Fit and style

- **Earbuds** that sit in the ear block some outside noise, which helps you hear the caller.
- **Open-ear** clip or hook designs, like the GP-W04 and GP-W06, let you hear the room too. That suits people who take calls while serving customers.

# Wired or wireless for calls?

**Wired** earphones with a mic never run out of battery and never need pairing. They are a dependable backup, as long as your phone has a 3.5mm headphone jack.

**Wireless** earbuds let you walk around during a call and leave the phone on the desk. Keep the phone within range. Our earbud listings state 10 m, but walls and pockets shorten it.

# Tips to sound clearer on any earphones

- **Wear them properly.** Earbud microphones are usually placed to point towards your mouth. If the earbud is twisted, the mic points the wrong way.
- **Do not cover the mic.** A collar, scarf or hand over a wired mic muffles your voice.
- **Turn away from the wind.** Wind across the mic is hard for any earphones to remove. Shelter behind a wall or turn your back to it.
- **Step away from the loudest noise.** Even a few metres from a generator or loudspeaker helps.
- **Speak normally.** Shouting does not help the mic; it often distorts.
- **Keep the phone close.** A weak Bluetooth link can make calls break up.
- **Check the call audio setting.** On Android, open the earbuds' Bluetooth settings and make sure call audio is switched on. On iPhone, check Call Audio Routing under Accessibility, then Touch.

# Keep the mic clean

Dust and fluff in the mic opening can muffle your voice. Wipe the earbuds with a soft, dry cloth and brush the openings gently with a soft, dry brush. Do not poke anything into them.
