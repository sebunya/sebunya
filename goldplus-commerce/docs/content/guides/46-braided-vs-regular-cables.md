A braided cable has a woven fabric-like jacket over the wires, while a regular cable has a smooth plastic (PVC) jacket. Braiding makes a cable tougher against kinks, scuffs and tangles. It does not, by itself, make your phone charge any faster. What decides speed is inside: the wires, the connectors and the cable's rating.

# What braiding does

The braid is a woven outer layer, usually nylon or a similar fibre, wrapped around the cable. It helps in a few practical ways:

- **Resists kinks.** It is harder to fold a braided cable into a sharp bend, which is what eventually breaks the wires inside.
- **Resists abrasion.** Rubbing against a bag zip, a car seat or a table edge wears through plain plastic faster.
- **Tangles less.** A braided cable tends to lie straighter in a pocket or bag.
- **Feels sturdier** in the hand, and it often looks better after months of use.

# What braiding does not do

- **It does not increase charging speed.** A braided cable and a plain cable with the same wires and rating charge at the same speed.
- **It does not protect the plug.** Most cables fail where the plug meets the cable. That joint needs a good strain-relief collar, braided or not.
- **It does not guarantee quality inside.** A cheap cable can have a nice braid over thin wires.

So read the rating and the connectors first, and treat the braid as a durability bonus.

# When a braided cable is worth it

- **You charge in bed or on the sofa,** where the cable gets bent, pulled and stepped on.
- **You carry the cable every day** in a bag or pocket.
- **You charge in the car or on a boda trip,** with the cable rubbing and bending as you move.
- **You keep losing cables to kinks** near the plug.

A plain PVC cable is fine for a cable that stays put, such as one left plugged in at a desk or by the bed where it is not handled much. It is also easy to wipe clean.

# Looking after either kind

- Pull from the plug, never from the cable.
- Do not wrap it tightly around a charger or your hand; loose loops are kinder.
- Keep it away from heat, such as a dashboard in the sun.
- A braid can fray at the ends over time. If the wires inside show, replace the cable.
