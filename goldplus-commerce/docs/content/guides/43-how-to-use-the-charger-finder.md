The [charger finder](/charger-finder) helps you match a charger to your phone, so you do not have to work out fast-charging standards and port types yourself. A minute of preparation makes it quicker: know your exact phone model and which port it has, and think about whether you charge one device or several.

# Why use it

Choosing a charger means matching several things at once:

- The **port** on your phone (USB-C, Lightning or Micro-USB).
- The **fast-charging standard** your phone uses (such as USB PD or Quick Charge).
- The **power** your phone can take.
- The **cable** that connects the charger's port to the phone.

Get one of these wrong and the phone still charges, but slowly. The charger finder brings these together for you, starting from the phone.

# Before you start

## Find your exact model

Open Settings, then About phone (on an iPhone, Settings, General, About). Note the model name and model number exactly. Two phones with similar names can charge differently.

## Look at the port

- **USB-C:** oval and reversible.
- **Lightning:** small and flat, on many iPhones before the iPhone 15.
- **Micro-USB (V8):** older, flat and only goes in one way.

## Think about how you charge

- Do you charge **one phone**, or a phone plus a second phone, a tablet or a laptop?
- Do you already have a **good cable**, or do you need one?

# Using the finder

Open the [charger finder](/charger-finder) and tell it about your phone. It suggests chargers that suit it. Then, for each suggestion:

1. **Open the product page** and read the listing: ports, fast-charging standard, output and what comes in the box.
2. **Check the cable.** Some chargers come with a cable, others do not. Make sure the cable's connectors match your charger's port and your phone's port.
3. **Check the ports for multi-device use.** On a dual-port charger, the power is shared when both are used.

# If your phone is not listed

Phones change every year, and new models appear all the time. If you cannot find yours:

- Read the label on the charger that came with the phone. It lists the output and often the standard.
- Search your exact model number for its charging specification.
- Send us your model number on WhatsApp on 0705 004545, or reach us through our [support page](/support), and we will help you match it.

# Before you order

- Read the output lines for the port you will use.
- Match the cable at both ends.
- If the box and the label disagree, ask us to read the exact unit.
