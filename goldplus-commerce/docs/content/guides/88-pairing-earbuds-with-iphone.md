To pair wireless earbuds with an iPhone, put the earbuds in pairing mode, open **Settings**, tap **Bluetooth**, and tap the earbuds' name under **Other Devices**. You do not need Apple earbuds for this: any standard Bluetooth earbuds work the same way.

# Before you start

- **Charge the earbuds and their case.** Earbuds with a flat battery will not appear.
- **Stay close.** Keep the iPhone within a metre or two while pairing.
- **Free them from other phones.** If the earbuds were last used with another phone nearby, switch that phone's Bluetooth off so it does not reconnect first.

# Step 1: put the earbuds in pairing mode

Many earbuds go into pairing mode when you open the case or take both earbuds out, and a light flashes. Some need you to press and hold the touch area or a button.

The exact method is in the manual that came with your earbuds. If you no longer have it, message us with the model and we will tell you.

# Step 2: pair from the iPhone

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Bluetooth** and make sure the switch is on (green).
3. Wait for your earbuds to appear under **Other Devices**.
4. Tap their name.

When the earbuds move up to **My Devices** and say **Connected**, pairing is done. Play a video to check you get sound in both ears.

> Turning Bluetooth off in Control Centre only disconnects devices for a while. To pair something new, use the Bluetooth page in Settings.

# If the earbuds do not show up

1. **Restart pairing mode.** Put both earbuds in the case, close the lid for a few seconds, then open it and try again.
2. **Turn Bluetooth off and on** in Settings.
3. **Charge them** for a while longer.
4. **Restart the iPhone.** This often clears a stuck Bluetooth connection.
5. **Forget the old pairing.** If the earbuds are already listed under My Devices but will not connect, tap the **i** next to their name, then **Forget This Device**. Then pair again from Step 1.

If they still do not appear, the earbuds may need resetting. How to do that differs between models, so follow the manual, or ask us.

# Settings worth checking after pairing

**Call audio.** When a call comes in, an iPhone normally uses the connected earbuds. If calls keep going to the phone's own speaker, check **Settings**, then **Accessibility**, then **Touch**, then **Call Audio Routing**, and make sure it is set to **Automatic**.

**Left and right balance.** If one side sounds quieter, look at **Settings**, then **Accessibility**, then **Audio & Visual**. Check the **Balance** slider is in the middle and that **Mono Audio** is set the way you want it.

**Headphone safety.** The iPhone has its own headphone volume tools under **Settings**, then **Sounds & Haptics**, then **Headphone Safety**. They can help you keep listening at a comfortable level.

Menu names can change a little between iOS versions. If you cannot find one, search for it with the search bar at the top of Settings.

# Things an iPhone may not show

Some earbuds show their battery level on an iPhone; others do not. If yours do not, use the battery lights on the case instead. Our [GP-W03](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp-w03) and [GP02BT](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp02bt) list an LED battery display on the case.

Features like automatic ear detection and the pop-up pairing card are mostly specific to Apple's own earbuds. Normal Bluetooth earbuds still play music and take calls on an iPhone without them.
