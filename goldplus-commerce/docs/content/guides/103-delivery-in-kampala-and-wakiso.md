GoldPlus delivers same-day in Kampala and Wakiso. Order before 5pm and your order goes out on that day's run; after that it goes on the next one. The delivery fee for your address is shown at checkout before you pay, and nothing is added after delivery.

Here is the detail.

# When we deliver

- **Delivery hours:** 8:30am to 8:00pm, Monday to Saturday.
- **The 5pm cut-off:** orders placed before 5pm go out on that day's run, on delivery days. Orders after 5pm go on the next run.
- **Arrival time:** when your order arrives depends on your area.

So if you order on a Saturday evening, your order goes out on the next delivery day's run, since there is no Sunday delivery.

# What delivery costs

The fee depends on your exact address and your order. There is no single flat price, which is why we do not print one here.

What you can rely on:

1. **You see an estimate before you pay.** Checkout shows the estimated fee for your address.
2. **It is confirmed before the rider leaves.**
3. **Nothing is added after delivery.**

To get the most accurate estimate, enter your address as precisely as you can at checkout.

# How you can pay

There are two ways, both offered at checkout.

**Pay straight away.** Use MTN or Airtel Mobile Money, or a Visa or Mastercard. We take you to PesaPal to finish, and your order is confirmed as soon as the money goes through.

**Pay on delivery or at our shop.** Order now and pay nothing today. We agree the delivery fee with you first, then you pay when your order reaches you, or when you collect it from our shop.

# Writing an address the rider can find

Many places in Kampala and Wakiso do not have street numbers, so landmarks matter. Name the area, the nearest well-known place, the building or gate, and the floor if it is not the ground floor. A short note such as "blue gate after the church, second house on the left" is more useful than a street name alone.

# Tips for a smooth delivery

- **Order before 5pm** if you need it the same day.
- **Give a clear address** and landmarks at checkout. It helps the rider and the fee estimate.
- **Keep your phone on.** The rider may need to reach you.
- **Check the fee** shown at checkout before you pay.
