The GoldPlus shop is on the **4th floor of the Zainab Aziza Building, Burton Street, Kampala**, opposite Pioneer Mall and next to Uhuru Restaurant. We are open **Monday to Saturday, 8:30am to 6:00pm**. You can call or WhatsApp us on **0705 004545**.

# Where we are

Zainab Aziza Building, 4th Floor, Burton Street, Kampala.

- **Landmarks:** opposite Pioneer Mall, next to Uhuru Restaurant.
- **Plus Code:** 8H7H+M5M Kampala. Type it into Google Maps to drop a pin right on the building.
- **Which side:** the shop is on the Burton Street side of the building, between Wilson Road and Burton Street.

A tip if you are giving directions to a boda rider: say "Zainab Aziza Building, Burton Street, opposite Pioneer Mall". Make sure it is Pioneer Mall, not a similarly named mall, so you end up on the right street.

# Why visit in person

- **See it before you buy.** Hold the power bank, try the size of the earbuds, look at the cable length.
- **Bring your old item.** If you are replacing something, such as a phone battery, bring the old one so you can compare codes and connectors on the spot.
- **Ask questions face to face.** Not sure which charger suits your phone? Ask.

# Make the trip worth it

A little preparation saves a second journey across town.

- **Know your phone model.** Open Settings, then About phone, and note the model exactly as shown. It is the quickest way to find the right battery, charger or cable.
- **Photograph labels.** A clear photo of the label on an old battery or charger is easier to share than describing it.
- **Browse first.** Look through our [shop](/shop) online and note the model codes of anything you want to see, so you can ask for it by name.
- **Bring the cable you use.** If you are unsure whether a charger or power bank suits your cable, having it with you makes comparing easier.
