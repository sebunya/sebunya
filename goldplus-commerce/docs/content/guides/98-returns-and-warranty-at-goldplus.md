There are two kinds of return at GoldPlus. If you have **changed your mind**, you have 14 days from delivery or collection to return the item unused and complete. If the product is **faulty**, it is replaced or refunded, and GoldPlus pays the cost of getting it back to us. Warranty claims go through support, and the coverage is whatever your product listing, receipt or the manufacturer states.

Here is each one in plain words.

# Changed your mind: 14 days

You have 14 days from delivery or collection to return an item you have changed your mind about.

To qualify, it must come back:

- **Unused.**
- **Complete:** the product, its packaging and everything that came in the box.
- **With your receipt or order confirmation.**

You can bring it to the shop, or send it back to us. For a change-of-mind return, the cost of getting the item back to us is yours.

Once we have it and it is complete, the refund goes back the way you paid.

# If the product is faulty

Different rules, and better ones. A faulty product is replaced or refunded, and **GoldPlus pays the cost of getting it back to us**.

Tell support what the fault is. We verify the purchase and the coverage on your listing or receipt, then agree the replacement or refund with you.

# How to start a return

**Online.** Start a return with your order number and the phone or email you used at checkout. Pick the item, tell us what happened, and our team calls you.

**Through support.** Contact GoldPlus support with your order number, the product involved and the reason for the return. The support team confirms the next steps, including whether the item should be brought to a GoldPlus location or collected.

Whichever way you start, keep the product, its packaging and your receipt or order confirmation until the return is resolved.

# Warranty: where the terms come from

Warranty coverage differs by product and brand. The coverage that applies to your purchase is the one stated on:

- the product listing,
- your receipt, or
- the manufacturer's documentation.

Our warranty page explains how claims work. It does not add to or replace those terms.

# How to make a warranty claim

Contact GoldPlus support with:

1. Your order number or receipt.
2. The product.
3. A description of the fault.

The support team verifies the purchase, checks the coverage that applies, and explains the assessment, repair or replacement steps.

# A quick checklist

- Keep your receipt or order confirmation.

- Note your order number. You need it to start a return or a claim, and you can find your order on the [track order](/track-order) page.
