When wireless earbuds will not connect, the cause is usually simple: a flat battery, an old pairing the phone is stuck on, or another device holding the connection. Work through the checks below in order. Most problems are fixed in the first three.

# 1. Charge the earbuds and the case

This is the most common cause. An earbud with too little charge can light up and still refuse to connect.

- Put both earbuds in the case and make sure they sit properly. They should click or settle into place.
- Charge the case from a working charger and cable. If your case has a battery display, check it shows it is charging.
- Look at the metal contacts on the earbuds and inside the case. Dust, earwax or pocket fluff can stop them charging. Clean them gently with a dry cotton bud.

Give them a proper charge before you go further.

# 2. Check something else is not connected

Earbuds usually reconnect to the last phone they used. If that phone, a laptop or a tablet is nearby with Bluetooth on, it may grab the earbuds first.

Switch Bluetooth off on the other devices, then try again.

# 3. Turn Bluetooth off and on, then restart the phone

1. Turn your phone's Bluetooth off, wait a few seconds, and turn it back on.
2. Put the earbuds in the case, close the lid, then open it again.
3. If that does not work, restart the phone completely.

A restart clears a lot of small Bluetooth glitches.

# 4. Forget the earbuds and pair them again

If the earbuds are in your phone's Bluetooth list but will not connect, the saved pairing may be faulty.

- **Android:** Settings, then Bluetooth. Tap the gear icon next to the earbuds and choose **Forget** or **Unpair**.
- **iPhone:** Settings, then Bluetooth. Tap the **i** next to the earbuds, then **Forget This Device**.

Now put the earbuds back in pairing mode and pair them as if they were new.

# 5. Reset the earbuds

Earbuds remember their own pairings too. A reset clears those and often fixes earbuds that connect one side only or keep dropping.

The reset method differs from model to model. It usually involves holding the touch area or a case button for a set time. Use the steps in the manual for your exact model. If you do not have the manual, message us with the model code and we will tell you.

After a reset, forget the earbuds on your phone (step 4) and pair again.

# 6. Rule out the phone

Try pairing the earbuds with a different phone. If they connect easily there, the problem is on your phone: check for a software update, or restart it again.

If the earbuds will not connect to any phone, even after charging and a reset, they may be faulty.

# If they connect but keep cutting out

That is a different problem from not connecting at all.

- **Keep the phone closer.** Our earbud listings state a range of 10 m, but walls and your own body shorten it. A phone in a back pocket or a bag on the other side can cause drop-outs.
- **Move away from crowded signals.** Busy places with lots of devices can interfere.
- **Charge them.** Low battery can cause drop-outs before the earbuds switch off.
