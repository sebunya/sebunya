Wireless earbuds run on very small batteries, so they only play for a few hours at a time. The charging case is what gets you through the day: it carries its own, bigger battery and tops the earbuds up whenever you put them back. To get the most from both, read the listing figures properly, keep the volume sensible and keep the case charged.

# Two batteries, two jobs

Look at a full earbud listing and you will usually see two battery sizes. Our [GP-W03](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp-w03), for example, lists:

- **Earbud battery:** 30mAh / 3.7V, in each earbud.
- **Charging case battery:** 230mAh / 3.7V.

The earbud battery decides how long you can listen in one go. The case battery decides how many times you can refill the earbuds before you need a socket.

The case is a small power bank shaped to hold the earbuds. When you close the lid, it charges them. When the case itself runs low, you charge it with a cable.

# Reading the figures on a listing

**Play time (or music time).** How long the earbuds play on one charge. The GP-W03 and [GP-W05](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp-w05) both list 4 hours. The [GP03BT](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp03bt) lists 6 hours of music at 80% volume.

**Talk time.** How long they last on calls. Calls use the microphone as well as the speaker, so this can differ from play time.

**Standby time.** How long they hold their charge while connected but not playing. The GP-W03 lists 100 hours. Standby is not listening time.

**Charging time.** How long a full recharge takes. The GP-W03 lists 1.5 hours; the [GP02BT](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp02bt) lists 1 hour.

Notice the conditions. When a listing says "at 80% volume", that is how the figure was measured. Listen louder and you will usually get less.

# Why your earbuds might not match the listing

- **Volume.** Louder listening drains the battery faster.
- **Calls.** Long calls use more power than music.
- **Distance.** Keeping the phone far away can make earbuds work harder to hold the connection.
- **Age.** Like every lithium-ion battery, earbud batteries slowly hold less charge over time and use.
- **Heat.** Batteries do not like being hot.

# Habits that help the batteries last

- **Put them back in the case.** It protects them and keeps them charged.
- **Keep the case charged too.** An empty case cannot refill anything. If your case has a battery display, glance at it before you head out. The GP-W03 and GP02BT list an LED display.
- **Keep them out of the heat.** Do not leave earbuds or the case on a car dashboard, in direct sun or near a stove.
- **Do not leave them flat for weeks.** If you will not use them for a while, give the case some charge first and top it up now and then.
- **Clean the contacts.** Dirt on the metal contacts can stop an earbud charging, which looks like a battery fault.
- **Use a working charger and cable.** The GP-W03, GP-W05 and [GP-W06](/products/goldplus-bluetooth-gp-w06) list a Type-C charging port. For any model, check the manual for how it should be charged.

# Charging on the go

For long days or trips upcountry, a small power bank can recharge the earbud case as well as your phone. Plug the case in during a break and you are set for the next stretch. See our [power banks](/power/power-banks).
