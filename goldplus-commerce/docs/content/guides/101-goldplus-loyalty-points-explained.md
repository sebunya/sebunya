GoldPlus Rewards is our loyalty programme, and it is live. Every order you place earns 10 points for every 1,000 UGX of your order total. The points become available when the order is delivered, and each point is worth UGX 20 off a later order at checkout.

Here is how it works in plain terms.

# Earning on your orders

- You earn **10 points per 1,000 UGX** of the order total.
- It applies to every order **placed while signed in**, and to orders placed **signed out with the phone number you verified** on your account.
- Verify your phone later and your recent signed-out orders on that number are credited too.
- Points become available **when the order is delivered**.

# What a point is worth

Each point is worth **UGX 20**, used as a discount at checkout.

- You can start using points **from 500 points**.
- Points can pay for **up to 50% of the goods total** of an order.
- Points are a discount, **never cash**.

# A worked example

An order totalling UGX 200,000 earns 2,000 points when it is delivered. At UGX 20 a point, that is UGX 40,000 off a later order: 20% back on what you spent.

Because points can pay for up to half of an order's goods, using all 2,000 points in one go needs an order with at least UGX 80,000 of goods.

# How long points last

Points stay valid for **120 days** from the day they are earned. We warn you before anything expires, and your oldest points are always spent first.

# Every other way to earn

The programme page lists the complete set. If a way to earn is not on that page, it does not exist yet.

- **Every delivered order:** 10 points per 1,000 UGX, signed in or placed with your verified phone number.
- **Verify a genuine product:** 25 points per code. Scan the hologram code on a GoldPlus product while signed in. Each code earns once, the first time it is verified, for up to 5 codes a day.
- **Report a counterfeit we confirm:** 250 points, awarded when our team confirms it. Support follows up with you.
- **Verify your phone number:** 100 points, once. Your verified number is how we keep one account per customer.
- **Introduce a friend:** 200 points when their first order is delivered, and they get 100 points too. Send your link; there is no code to type.
- **Introduce friends and family:** a one-off 300-point bonus when the third friend you introduced has their first order delivered, on top of the points for each friend.
- **Keep a delivery streak:** 300 points for 3 delivered orders in a row, each within 90 days of the last.
- **Five deliveries:** a one-off 250-point bonus and the Loyal Customer badge when five of your orders have been delivered.
- **Your birthday:** 150 points once a year, when you add your date of birth to your account.

# A scratch card with every delivery

Every delivered order also brings a scratch card, free and on top of the points the order already earned. Every card wins; only the amount varies, and the odds are published in the [loyalty terms](/loyalty-terms). Cards expire after 30 days, so scratch yours soon.

# Fair-use limits

Verification and reporting have earning caps so the programme stays fair. Scans are capped per day, and a code can only ever earn once.

# Membership levels

There are also membership levels, earned on the points you collect. The [loyalty page](/loyalty) explains how they work.

# Making the most of it

1. Create an account and **sign in before you order**, or check out with your verified phone number.
2. **Verify your phone number** for a quick 100 points.
3. **Add your birthday** to your account.
4. **Share your link** with friends who need accessories.
5. Keep an eye on expiry, and use points on an order with enough goods to make the most of the 50% limit.
