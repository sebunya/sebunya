To pair wireless earbuds with an Android phone, charge them, put them in pairing mode, then open Bluetooth on your phone and tap the earbuds' name when it appears. After the first time, they should reconnect on their own whenever you take them out of the case.

Menu names differ a little between Tecno, Infinix, itel, Samsung and other phones, but the steps are the same.

# Before you start

- **Charge the earbuds and the case.** A flat earbud will not show up. Put the earbuds in the case and charge it for a while first.
- **Keep the phone close.** Stand within a metre or two while pairing.
- **Disconnect other devices.** If the earbuds were paired to another phone nearby, switch Bluetooth off on that phone, or it may grab the connection first.

# Step 1: put the earbuds in pairing mode

On many earbuds, opening the case or taking both earbuds out puts them in pairing mode, and a light flashes to show they are ready. Some models need you to press and hold the touch area or a button on the case.

How your pair does it is in the manual that came in the box. If you have lost it, ask us and we will tell you for your model.

# Step 2: open Bluetooth on your phone

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Bluetooth**, or **Connected devices** and then **Bluetooth** on some phones.
3. Turn Bluetooth on if it is off.
4. Tap **Pair new device** if you see it. On some phones the list of available devices appears straight away.

A shortcut: swipe down from the top of the screen and press and hold the Bluetooth icon. This opens the same screen on most Android phones.

# Step 3: tap the earbuds' name

Wait a few seconds for the list to fill. Your earbuds usually appear under their model name, for example something that includes the model code printed on the box.

Tap the name, then tap **Pair** if the phone asks. When the status changes to **Connected**, you are done. Play a song or a video to check sound comes through both sides.

# If the earbuds do not appear

1. **Refresh the list.** Turn the phone's Bluetooth off and on again, or close and reopen the Bluetooth screen.
2. **Restart pairing mode.** Put both earbuds back in the case, close it for a few seconds, then open it again.
3. **Check the battery.** Put them back on charge and try again after a while.
4. **Look for an old pairing.** If the earbuds' name is already in your "previously connected" list but will not connect, tap the gear icon next to it, choose **Forget** or **Unpair**, and pair again from Step 1.
5. **Restart the phone.** It clears small Bluetooth glitches more often than you would expect.

# After pairing: settings worth checking

- **Media and call audio.** Tap the gear icon next to the earbuds. Make sure both calls and media audio are switched on, so calls come through the earbuds as well as music.
- **Rename them.** Some phones let you rename the earbuds here, which helps if a family shares several pairs.
- **Volume.** The phone and the earbuds can both control volume. If it seems quiet, turn up the phone's media volume while the earbuds are playing.

> If the phone asks for a PIN when pairing, check the manual. Many earbuds do not need one, and some use a simple default code given in the manual.
