A phone battery is made for one phone, or a small family of phones. Two batteries can look the same and still not fit, or fit and charge wrongly. The way to get it right is to match the exact model, not the brand and not the size.

# Step 1: find your exact model number

Your model number is more precise than the name on the box. "Tecno Spark 10" can cover more than one version, but the model number tells them apart.

- **In Settings:** open Settings, then About phone. The model and model number are listed there.
- **On the phone:** it is often printed on the back of the phone or under the battery cover.
- **What it looks like:** a short code such as KF6n, X695 or SM-A326B.

Write it down exactly as shown, including any letters at the end.

# Step 2: find the battery code, if you can see it

On phones with a removable back, the battery has its own code printed on its label.

- Tecno, Infinix and itel battery codes usually start with **BL-**, for example BL-49FV.
- Samsung battery codes usually start with **EB-**.
- iPhone batteries are specific to each model, so the exact model (for example iPhone 11 or iPhone 11 Pro) is what matters.

The battery code is the most reliable thing to match. If the code on your old battery and the code on the new one are the same, you are on the right track.

> Never open a sealed phone to find a code, and never remove or charge a battery that is swollen or damaged. Your model number in Settings is enough for us to help.

# Signs your battery needs replacing

- The phone dies long before the end of the day, even with light use.
- It switches off suddenly at 20% or 30%.
- It gets unusually hot while charging.
- The back cover bulges or the screen lifts at the edge. This is a swollen battery: stop using and charging the phone, keep it away from heat, and have it looked at.

# Match the code, not the shape

A battery with the same size or the same mAh but a different code is not a safe swap. The connector, the voltage and the way the phone manages charging can all differ. If the codes do not match, ask us first.
