When an Android phone's battery drains fast, start with the battery usage screen. It shows what is using your charge. Often it is one app, the screen, location or a weak signal, and a few setting changes fix it. If nothing stands out and the phone is a couple of years old, the battery itself is probably worn.

Menu names differ between brands such as Tecno, Infinix, itel and Samsung, and between Android versions. If you cannot find a setting where we describe it, use the search bar at the top of Settings.

# 1. Look at battery usage

Open **Settings**, then **Battery**, then **Battery usage** (or similar).

- If one app uses far more than the others, open it from that list and look for options to restrict its background use.
- If a game or video app is at the top, that is expected. If an app you hardly open is near the top, it is running in the background.
- If "Screen" or "Display" is at the top, see step 2.
- If "Mobile network" or "Phone idle" is high, see step 5.

# 2. Screen settings

The screen is often the biggest drain.

- **Brightness:** turn on adaptive or auto-brightness, or lower it.
- **Screen timeout:** set it to 30 seconds or one minute.
- **Refresh rate:** if your phone offers 90Hz or 120Hz, the standard rate uses less power. Look under Display.
- **Dark mode:** on phones with OLED or AMOLED screens, dark mode can save power. On LCD screens it makes little difference.
- **Always-on display:** turn it off if you do not use it.

# 3. Location

Open **Settings**, then **Location**.

- See which apps have location permission. Change apps that do not need it to "Only while using" or "Don't allow".
- Turn off Wi-Fi and Bluetooth scanning if you do not need them. These let the phone search for networks and devices even when Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are off.

# 4. Background apps and notifications

- **Battery optimisation:** under Settings, then Battery, many phones let you set apps to "Optimised" or "Restricted" in the background. Leave messaging apps you rely on unrestricted, or you may miss messages.
- **Adaptive battery:** if your phone has this option, turn it on. It learns which apps you use and limits the rest.
- **Notifications:** apps that buzz all day wake the screen and the phone. Turn off ones you do not need.
- **Uninstall apps you no longer use.**

# 5. Network and signal

- **Weak signal:** the phone raises its radio power to stay connected. In areas with poor coverage, the battery drains faster. If you are somewhere with no signal for hours, turn on airplane mode.
- **Mobile data vs Wi-Fi:** use reliable Wi-Fi where you have it.
- **5G:** if your phone and area support 5G but coverage is patchy, switching to 4G/LTE under the network settings can reduce drain.
- **Hotspot:** sharing data from your phone uses a lot of battery. Turn it off when you finish.

# 6. Software

- **Restart the phone.** A stuck process can drain a healthy battery.
- **Check for updates** under Settings, then System (or About phone). After an update, give it a day or two to settle.
- **Battery saver:** use it when you need to stretch the last 20%, during a power cut or on a long trip.

# 7. Is it the battery?

If you have been through these steps and the phone still:

- does not last your normal day,
- switches off at 20% or 30%,
- gets hot while idle, or
- has a lifting back or screen,

then the battery is likely worn. A lifting back or screen means a swollen battery: stop using and charging it straight away.
