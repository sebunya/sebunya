# Telemetry and Identity Enrichment Architecture

This document describes how GoldPlus Commerce OS stitches browser sessions, guest checkouts, and customer profiles into a secure, privacy-compliant identity graph for telemetry and downstream ad-network attribution.

---

## 1. Hybrid Hashing Strategy
Ad networks (Meta Conversion API, Google Ads, TikTok Pixel) require identity parameters to be sent as plain SHA-256 hashes of normalized inputs to match users in their identity graphs.
- **HMAC-SHA256 (Internal Database)**: We encrypt PII in internal records using HMAC-SHA256 with a pepper key for high security.
- **Plain SHA-256 (Outbound Sinks)**: We compute un-peppered SHA-256 hashes for outbound telemetry packets.

### Normalization Logic
To ensure maximum match rates across channels:
- **Email**: Stripped of leading/trailing whitespace, converted to lowercase, and all internal spaces are removed.
- **Phone**: Formatted using the international E.164 standard (e.g. `+2567...`), stripped of all non-numeric characters (except the leading `+`), and hashed.

---

## 2. Browser Identity Stitching Flow
The `StitchBrowserIdentityUseCase.ts` combines frontend cookie identifiers with backend identifiers:
1. **Client Identification**: Frontend passes a transient `clientId` (from a cookie or localStorage) and a session token.
2. **Identification Event**: When the customer registers, logs in, or submits an order, their email and phone are captured and hashed using both formats.
3. **Graph Stitching**: The system binds the `clientId` and `customerId` in the `first_party_identities` table.
4. **Retroactive Attribution**: Future events generated by that browser session are automatically decorated with standard SHA-256 email/phone hashes, ensuring offline purchases are matched to web-ad click actions.

---

## 3. Signal Quality Scoring
The purchase-time scorer this section used to describe (`EnqueuePurchaseEventUseCase.ts` and
`infrastructure/telemetry/PurchaseTelemetry.ts`) has been removed. Purchases now come from ONE
source: the authoritative `order_confirmed` event that the order transition appends in the same
transaction (migration 0140), carried out by durable delivery intents.

Match quality is read from `attribution_touchpoints.match_score` and summarised for the admin
Measurement pages by `GET /admin/measurement/match-quality` (aggregated in SQL). With no
conversion events in the window the summary is "no data" (null), never 0%.
