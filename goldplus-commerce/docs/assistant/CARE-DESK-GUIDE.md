# The Care Desk: a guide for the shop team

Open **shopgoldplus.com/admin/care** and sign in. Keep it open on the shop computer; it refreshes by itself every
minute.

## What you see
- **At the top:** how many customers are waiting for you (urgent, call backs, changes to approve, bulk buyers), and
  how many customers Goldie helped today.
- **From the last shift:** notes the previous shift left you. Leave your own before you go home.
- **Waiting for a person:** each card is one customer. Red at the top means urgent. The coloured edge shows how long
  they have waited: green is fine, amber is getting long (over 30 minutes), red is too long (over 2 hours).

## Working a card
1. Press **I'll take it**, so others know you have it.
2. **Call** or **WhatsApp** the customer. The buttons dial the number for you.
3. **Read the chat** to see everything the customer told Goldie, so you don't ask again.
4. Write a short note: "Called, no answer", "Will deliver at 4pm".
5. When it's sorted, write what you did and press **Done**. If it's no longer needed, press **Not needed**.

## Kinds of card
- **Urgent:** a customer is upset or stuck (money, a missing order, a fault). Take these first.
- **Call back:** the customer asked to be called. Call today.
- **Order problem:** late, wrong or damaged. Check the order, sort it out, and tell the customer.
- **Approve a change:** cancel, change the address or time. Call the customer to confirm, make the change in the
  order, then press Done.
- **Bulk buyer:** a reseller wants a quote. Send the bulk price the same day.
- **Follow up:** anything else Goldie passed to you.

## Replying from a conversation
On the conversation page, choose a **saved reply** to start from, edit it, and send:
- a WhatsApp chat goes out on WhatsApp, and Goldie stays quiet for 24 hours while you handle it;
- a website chat with an email goes out by email.

Use **Take it over** if you want Goldie to stay quiet longer.

## Alerts on your phone
If you are on the team list, urgent customers reach you by SMS and email. If nobody takes an urgent customer in 15
minutes, everyone is alerted again.
