# Draft for the privacy policy (legal review before publishing)

**Chatting with Goldie.** When you chat with Goldie, our virtual personal shopper, on our website or on WhatsApp,
we keep the conversation so our team can follow up and so we can improve our service. We remove card numbers, PINs,
one-time codes and national ID numbers from what we keep. We keep conversations for 365 days, then delete them.
If you give Goldie your phone number or email, or you are signed in, we link the conversation to you so we can reply
and so it appears if you ask for a copy of your data. You can ask for a copy of your conversations, or for them to
be deleted, through your account (Privacy) or by contacting us.

**What Goldie remembers about your interests.** If you allow personalisation, Goldie notes the kind of products you
asked about (for example "power banks") to help you faster next time. She never stores payment details for this.

**Answers from an AI system.** Goldie's answers are produced by software from our product, order and delivery
information; prices and stock are checked live. If you prefer a person, the team is available on WhatsApp or can call
you back.
