### Deeper inspection (8 pages, compared like for like)

**1. KWT Tech Mart — Phone Chargers collection**
- URL: https://kwttechmart.ug/collections/phone-chargers — 2026-10-28
- Intent: "phone charger Kampala/Uganda" (commercial). Audience: Kampala online buyer. Type: category.
- Strengths: 8 in-stock items with UGX prices (Oraimo OCW-U63D UGX 27,000 to Powerology 10k wall-charger bank UGX 280,800); collection text advises matching wattage, port count, certified safety, and notes Ugandan voltage variation; CoD in Kampala, MoMo/bank for upcountry; 7-day returns.
- Weaknesses: specs only where the title carries them; no compatibility, no certifications, no reviews, warranty "varies by product"; brand filter spelled "Powerlogy".
- Evidence: manufacturer-stated. Local relevance: high (Kampala/upcountry logistics). Purchase path: cart, CoD/MoMo.
- Lesson: short category-level buying guidance plus explicit delivery/payment terms ranks this page for 9 of 25 queries.
- GoldPlus fix: add a 120-word "how to choose" block and per-card W/ports/compatibility to each category page (currently none have any).

**2. KWT Tech Mart — "Phone Battery Draining Fast in Uganda" guide**

- Lesson: Uganda-specific problem content from a retailer ranks for 5 of the battery queries.

## PART 3 — Synthesis

1. In 25 queries shopgoldplus.com surfaced once, only when the brand name and street were typed. Nothing category-, model- or problem-shaped reaches it.
2. The queries that would convert a battery buyer (#3, #4, #6, #12, #18) return no Ugandan seller at all; Kenyan odoo shops and iFixit forums fill the vacuum.
3. KWT Tech Mart wins Ugandan visibility with a thin catalogue plus Uganda-specific problem articles; it does not sell batteries and routes readers to Carlcare.
4. Price-anchor pages with the literal phrase "... price in Uganda" (Nabellas) rank despite duplicate listings and missing payment info.
5. A direct neighbour (Mutoni Phone Hub, New Pioneer Mall) publishes 2026 local price guides whose bands sit above GoldPlus's actual prices.
6. Oraimo is the volume brand (398 Jiji power-bank ads, Uganda store, Carlcare service) and undercuts GoldPlus's 20k bank by ~2x on flash sale.
7. "GoldPlus" is already being resold on Jiji/SangZ/Jumia by third parties at prices and capacities GoldPlus's own site does not state (GP-X01 "80,000 mAh" at USh 180,000 vs UGX 260,000 own price).
8. No Ugandan or regional site shows a measured number for any accessory; everything observed is manufacturer-stated or unsourced.
9. The brand name collides with Titan's "GoldPlus" jewellery in generic search.
10. shopgoldplus.com's own catalogue has no filters, no Wh, no reviews, no guides, and JSON-LD covers 60 of 77 products.
11. Jumia Uganda is alive (June 2026 coverage); Kilimall Uganda is relaunching (site live in UGX, 2025-26 hiring); both block scraping, neither ranked for product queries.
