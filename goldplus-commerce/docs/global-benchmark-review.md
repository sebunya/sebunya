# Global Benchmark Review: Principles Extraction

## Purpose
This document logs the extracted principles from global e-commerce benchmark websites. Under strict anti-hallucination and brand originality rules, no layouts, copy, images, product claims, icons, or proprietary patterns from benchmarks will be copied. 

## Extracted Principles

1. **Information Density & Hierarchy**
   - High-contrast primary action buttons.
   - Concise product names with prioritized technical specifications (e.g. wattage, battery life) listed immediately below.
   - Clear pricing hierarchy (retail price vs. wholesale/dealer pricing must remain separated per security boundaries).

2. **Mobile-First Interaction Patterns**
   - Bottom-heavy interactions on mobile devices (e.g. sticky bottom "Add to Cart" bars on product pages).
   - Horizontal scrolling carousels for category browsing to save vertical screen space.
   - Large, thumb-friendly tap targets (minimum 44x44px).

3. **Trust & Performance Signaling**
   - Instant perceived load times (leveraging our strict PWA cache rules).
   - Clear warranty, return policies, and local support contact information prominently displayed to build trust in the Ugandan market.
   - Genuine, verified product claims without fake scarcity.

4. **Visual Simplicity (Premium Feel)**
   - Extensive use of whitespace to let product details breathe.
   - Restrained use of brand colors. The brand's primary green and gold should be used for emphasis and trust, grounded by black and white for readability and structure.
