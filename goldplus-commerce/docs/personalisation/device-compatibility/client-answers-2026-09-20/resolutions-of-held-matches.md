# The seven held matches — resolved from the client's answers and public parts data (2026-09-20)

The client was offline; each decision below can be reversed by a GoldPlus packaging photo or a technician's fit check. Seller listings are leads, not proof — every staged row is still a supplier-listed DRAFT.

| # | Held match | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BL-4UL → Nokia **Asha 500** | **Rejected.** Stays on BL-4U. | Nokia's published spec for the Asha 500 is BL-4U 1200 mAh (Wikipedia, DeviceSpecifications); BL-4U and BL-4UL are not interchangeable (LPC wiki). The client's own BL-4U answer already lists the 500/501/503 together. Some eBay sellers title BL-4UL packs "Asha 500" — seller noise. |
| 2 | VIVO B-D2 → **X20** | **Rejected.** | B-D1 (≈3245 mAh) is the X20 / X20A pack; B-D2 (3905 mAh) is the X20 Plus family — consistent across BatteryClub, Indclues, BatteryGod, Batteriestar, Energystox. Different capacity and size. |
| 3 | VIVO B-B1 → **Y55s** | **Staged as "Y55s (2017)", model 1610.** | Alibaba ("Y55S Y55L 1610 1603 B-B1 2730 mAh"), Virateck, Kaeel, ZeenKart and RickGSM all list the 2017 Y55s on B-B1 with the Y55/Y55L/Y55A. Naming the year and model keeps the 2021+ Y55s 5G from ever matching. |
| 4 | A11/BLP727 → **Realme C25** | **Rejected.** | The C25 (RMX3191/RMX3193) takes BLP793, **6000 mAh** — every source agrees. The A11/BLP727 battery is a 5000 mAh-class pack; a different size class cannot be the same pack. |
| 5 | A11/BLP727 → **Realme 6i** | **Staged for the global RMX2040 only, with a caution.** | Its own pack is BLP771, 5000 mAh — the same OPPO/Realme 5000 mAh class as BLP727 (OPPO A5 2020) and BLP729 (Realme 5). The client trades these and lists them on one GoldPlus battery, exactly as for the Realme 5 / C3 already staged. Needs a fit check to be *verified*. The India RMX2002 (4300 mAh, different pack) is excluded by naming the model number. |
| 6 | BL-24ET → **"Pop 2 Go"** | **Not a new phone — an alias of the TECNO Pop 2 (B1).** | The Pop 2 family ships "Android 8.1 (Go edition)"; "Pop 2 Go" is the trade's name for it. BL-24ET is the Pop 1 / Pop 2 / Pop 2F pack (gsm-forsage, Alibaba, Daraz), and the Pop 2 (B1) is already staged on BL-24ET. Creating "Pop 2 Go" would duplicate it. Staff add the alias to the phone. |
| 7 | BL-38BT → **Pop 2 Go** | **Rejected** (by the client). | Client: BL-38BT is for the Pop 5 Go and Pop 6 Go; the Pop 2 Go uses BL-24ET. |
