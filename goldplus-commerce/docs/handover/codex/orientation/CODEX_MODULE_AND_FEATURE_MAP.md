## Shared dependency direction

Commerce/order facts feed inventory, fulfilment, notification, Customer DNA, measurement, and Decision Intelligence. Customer DNA and consent constrain NBA and Automation. Decision Intelligence can propose a draft Automation handoff but cannot activate a version. Automation planning produces internal plans; A3 must pass eligibility/suppression/frequency gates and persist internal or outbox effects before any existing provider router can be involved. RBAC, audit, logging, metrics, redaction, PostgreSQL, Registry, and deployment files are cross-cutting and therefore higher blast radius than a bounded domain addition.
