# GoldPlus Next Best Action Standard

The Next Best Action is **the most useful, logical and honest action for this
customer in this state** — not the click GoldPlus most wants. Conversion
obtained through confusion is a defect.

## 1. Every meaningful state carries

| | |
|---|---|
| Primary action | the one obvious next step; on mobile, one dominant button |
| Secondary action | a useful alternative, visibly secondary |
| Escape | help, back or cancel — never hidden to reduce support volume |
| Consequence | the customer knows what happens after the tap |
| Recovery | if it fails, what happened, what it means, what to do |

Choice overload is friction: never four equal pills. One dominant action.

## 2. The four phases of a critical action

**Before** — enough information to act (price, fee, what the button does).
**During** — a real disabled state and a plain label (*Sending your order…*);
never a still-clickable button dressed as disabled.
**After success** — confirm what happened, show the reference, name the next
step, and say how we will contact them.
**After failure** — what failed, the money consequence, whether to retry, and
a person to ask.

## 3. The critical matrix

| State | Dominant question | Primary | Secondary | Escape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Product in stock | can I buy this? | Add to cart | Buy now | ask on WhatsApp |
| Product out of stock | when can I get it? | Ask us on WhatsApp | see similar | back to shop |
| Cart with items | what will this cost? | Continue to checkout | Continue shopping | edit lines |
| Cart empty | where do I start? | Shop all products | popular now | — |
| Zero search results | do you have it at all? | Ask us for "X" | clear search | see what is in stock |
| Checkout, online pay | will this charge me? | Pay for this order | Edit cart | — |
| Checkout, pay later | what happens next? | Place order | Edit cart | — |
| Checkout refused | was I charged? | (message says no) place again | Edit cart | contact us |
| Payment success | is it done? | Track this order | Continue shopping | — |
| Payment pending / review / unknown | did my money go? | Check this order | Back to the shop | WhatsApp / call |
| Payment failed | am I out of pocket? | Try payment again (no duplicate) | Back to the shop | WhatsApp / call |
| Payment cancelled | does my order exist? | Pay for this order | Back to the shop | WhatsApp / call |
| Order unpaid | how do I pay? | Track this order | Ask about this order | WhatsApp / call |

| Offline | is my order safe? | Retry | — | — |

## 4. Rules

- **Financial actions** name the amount or the page: *Pay for this order*,
  never *Pay securely now* for a redirect.
- **Destructive actions** name the object: *Remove item*, and confirm what
  changed afterwards.
- **Retry** is offered only when it cannot duplicate an order or charge, and
  the copy says so. Pending states say *do not pay again*.
- **Pending** is amber, never red. Waiting is not failure.
- **Success** confirms, gives the reference, and names the contact channel
  that actually works (phone/WhatsApp — not email until it delivers).
- **Dead ends** are defects: a reachable state with no forward, back or help
  action fails review.
- **Returning customers** confirm, they do not retype: saved addresses and
  the retained checkout intent are part of the action design.
- **Mobile first:** one dominant action visible without scrolling; 44px
  targets; the sticky header must not cover it.

## 5. Ownership

A new customer state is not done until its row in the matrix above exists
and its recovery path has been walked in a real browser.
